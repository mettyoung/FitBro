## 2026-06-10 — US-002: Chart plots the active view's value

- Learnings: positive-only bars reuse the top-half path so they occupy only the upper half of chart height (zero line stays centered) — matches "upward-only" intent. Keep the 100..3000 clamp scoped to diverging; applying it to intake/burn would flatten all bars to max.
